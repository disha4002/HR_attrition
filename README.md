HR Attrition

Project Title and Description:
The HR Attrition is an interactive dashboard made using Power BI (Power query, data modelling and DAX measures). The dashboard analyses the Attrition rate of the employees from a company.
In this I have analyzed the attrition rate based on the factors such as gender, martial status, overtime and business travel.
It solves the business problem of understanding the and finding the reasons that why are employees leaving?
