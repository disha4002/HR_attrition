## 📊 HR Attrition Dashboard

## 📌 Project Title and Description:

The HR Attrition is an interactive dashboard made using Power BI (Power query, data modelling and DAX measures). The dashboard analyses the Attrition rate of the employees from a company.
In this I have analyzed the attrition rate based on the factors such as gender, martial status, overtime and business travel.
It solves the business problem of understanding the and finding the reasons that why are employees leaving?

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
Key technologies which I have used to build the dashboard
Power BI Desktop: The main visualization platform to build the dashboard.
Power Query: Power Query is a crucial step before visualizing data to make sure the data is clean and transformed in a structured format, so that we can easily do dashboard creation.
DAX (Data Analysis Expression): DAX was used for calculated measures and conditional logic.
File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard previews.

## 📂 Data Source
Source- The data was collected from Kaggle. 
The dataset consists of 1,470 rows and 35 columns about with the information about employee age, salary, marital status, attrition rate, business travels, department education and so on.

## 🧠Features/ Highlights
Walkthrough of key visuals
KPI's- There are total five KPI's representing total attrition, employees left, total headcounts, average income and average tenure of leavers(total working years of employees in the company before leaving)
Slicer- Used for making the navigation through dashboard easier. 
Stacked Bar Chart- shows attrition rate per department
Vertical Bar Chart- Each bar chart represents different category- attrition rate by gender, attrition rate by martial status, attrition rate by business travel and by overtime.

## 💼 Business Impact

The HR Attrition Dashboard enables HR teams and business leaders to monitor employee turnover and identify the key factors contributing to attrition. By analyzing trends across departments, job roles, age groups, salary slabs, and education fields, the dashboard supports data-driven workforce planning and employee retention strategies.

The insights generated from this dashboard can help organizations:

- Reduce employee attrition by identifying high-risk groups.
- Improve retention strategies through targeted HR interventions.
- Support workforce planning and recruitment decisions.
- Monitor department-wise attrition to allocate HR resources effectively.
- Enable faster decision-making using interactive visualizations instead of manual reporting.
